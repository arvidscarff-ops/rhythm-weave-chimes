## 1. How to read this index

This document exists to prevent brainstorming, evocative assistant suggestions, and abandoned terminology from silently becoming canon.

### Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LOCKED / CANON** | Explicitly selected, repeatedly affirmed, or stated by the creator as a foundational rule. Later documents may clarify it but should not contradict it without a recorded decision. |
| **PLANNED** | Intended direction or system with meaningful creator endorsement, but details, scope, implementation, or release timing remain open. |
| **EXPLORATORY** | Brainstormed, proposed, or positively received, but not selected as current canon. |
| **UNRESOLVED** | The conversation raises incompatible alternatives or lacks enough evidence to choose safely. |
| **SUPERSEDED / REJECTED** | Explicitly rejected, corrected, replaced by later terminology, or ruled out as the current direction. |

### Evidence rules used in this run

1. A direct user decision outranks an assistant proposal.
2. A repeated user premise outranks a single speculative exchange.
3. Assistant enthusiasm does not establish canon by itself.
4. “Could,” “maybe,” “what if,” and feature lists labelled potential or long-term remain non-canon unless subsequently adopted.
5. A name chosen for an institution does not automatically become the project, world, era, or game title.
6. Fictional truth, in-world belief, product intent, and implementation state are separate categories.
7. When the available history cannot prove that an idea was adopted, it is marked **UNRESOLVED** or **EXPLORATORY**, not promoted.

### 2.2 Naming drift that must remain visible

- **Kashmir Altara → Kashmer-Alterra:** The earlier phonetic idea was explicitly challenged by the creator because of real-world/place associations and prior sci-fi usage. “Kashmer-Alterra” remains useful as a working corpus label, but final naming is unresolved.
- **Pilot → Navigator:** “Pilot” remains useful in plain-language explanations, but **Navigator** is the intended in-world term.
- **Navigator → Attuned / Meridian:** “Attuned” was proposed by the assistant and not selected. “Meridian” became much stronger, but its exact ontology—rank, title, recognized state, profession-adjacent class, or corridor—is unresolved.
- **Meridian as company → Meridian as phenomenon → Meridian as person/state:** All three usages appeared. They may coexist, but the relationship must be deliberately defined in a later lore/game-systems decision.
- **Weather → Atmospheric Harmony / Harmonic Climate:** The creator locked a world-scale persistent weather mechanic; the more metaphysical names and expanded phenomena are elaborations, not yet replacements.
- **The Event / Convergence / Drift / Bloom / Long Weather:** These are not interchangeable canon. “The Convergence” is the strongest working catastrophe term; “The Long Weather” and related phrases belong primarily to title/folk-language exploration.
- **World title / game title / era title:** The conversation deliberately explored separating these. No final separation or title set was selected.

## 3. LOCKED / CANON

### 3.1 Product identity and experience

1. **PHASE is an audiovisual interactive work with game, ambient, focus, meditation, and background-music qualities.**
   - It should support active attention and low-demand/background use.
   - Progression should remain low-dopamine rather than relying on constant high-intensity rewards.
   - The experience is not defined by a conventional victory condition.

2. **Transit is the central experiential unit.**
   - A crossing is not fast travel.
   - It unfolds as a long-form audiovisual composition.
   - The player travels from a cockpit/pilot-point-of-view through skies, clouds, changing weather, and anomalous conditions.
   - Long duration is part of the intended identity, not automatically a pacing defect.

3. **The world and instrument are one design.**
   - Rhythm, sound, visual geometry, transit duration, environmental conditions, and fictional systems are intended to reinforce one another.
   - Audiovisual complexity and presentation may evolve with player progression.

4. **Emotional north star: minimal but persistent hope.**
   - Humanity has only enough hope to continue.
   - Civilization persists through routine: people keep flying, repairing, transmitting, cooking, cataloguing, and crossing without certainty that recovery is possible.
   - The setting is not nihilistic; things matter even when success is doubtful.

5. **Horror must be beautiful, deeply frightening, and unsettling.**
   - Daylight horror is central.
   - Beauty is part of the horror, not merely a contrast to it.
   - The preferred fear comes from reality behaving consistently according to incomprehensible rules, not primarily from gore, darkness, or jump scares.

6. **The social-emotional ideal includes “joint solitude.”**
   - Players may feel connected across distance without destroying isolation or contemplation.
   - Social design should preserve the compositional and solitary atmosphere.

7. **The generative audiovisual instrument is PHASE’s gateway experience.**
   - It must provide sufficient standalone value for listening, viewing, meditation, deep work, concentration, and rest.
   - Users may encounter the instrument before understanding the world, routes, MTC, progression, or lore.
   - World and game systems should be revealed as deeper layers rather than prerequisites for experiencing the instrument.

### 3.2 World foundations

1. **The setting is future Earth, approximately 130 years after an extraterrestrial arrival/contact event.**
2. **The arrival began the first Zones, which have since engulfed or transformed most of Earth.**
3. **Something associated with the visitors now permeates Earth.**
   - Its exact carrier is unresolved: ship, ship-borne material/system, alien biology, or another mechanism.
4. **The phenomenon makes matter—and potentially space and time—susceptible to manipulation or transformation through harmony and rhythm.**
5. **Alteration is better understood creatively as convergence/transformation than simple rot or corruption.**
   - Things become new stable or semi-stable versions of themselves.
   - Outcomes may be anomalous, horrific, beautiful, or all three.
6. **Human civilization survives in fragmented settlements/regions dependent on difficult long-range transit.**
7. **Glider-based transit is essential to civilization’s continuity.**
8. **All notes/scales in the world’s musical system use a 432 Hz tuning reference.**
   - The precise technical interpretation—most plausibly A4 = 432 Hz—must be specified later; “every note equals 432 Hz” would be musically incoherent and is not the intended reading.

### 3.3 MTC and Navigators

1. **The institution is named Meridian Transit Corporation (MTC).**
2. **MTC is good/necessary rather than a secretly corrupt megacorporation.**
   - It belongs to a broadly “utopian apocalypse” register.
   - It may possess enormous power because civilization depends on it, but corruption is not its defining premise.
3. **MTC functions more like a public utility/civilizational institution than a conventional airline.**
   - It is associated with routes, gliders, training, certification, maintenance, navigation, and the infrastructure connecting humanity.
4. **MTC’s cultural identity should feel old, institutional, and continuous.**
   - The key formulation is a European scientific institution founded around the 1960s and surviving into roughly 2150.
   - Advanced technology coexists with inherited rituals, craft traditions, old equipment, markings, uniforms, manuals, tokens, and institutional memory.
5. **Navigators are not merely pilots.**
   - They survive and operate in conditions ordinary humans cannot reliably perceive or withstand.
   - Their work involves continuous sensitivity to resonance, atmospheric shifts, corridor stability, and anomaly pressure.
   - Calibration, breathing, listening, rhythm, and disciplined perception should read as technically necessary practices that outsiders may interpret as spiritual.
6. **Navigators receive near-sacred public regard because they are essential and rare, not because the setting declares them literal clergy.**

### 3.4 Technical and musical invariants already established for later documentation

The following are referenced by the approved documentation plan as pre-existing architectural decisions and should be treated as canon-level constraints pending exact code audit:

1. **Centralized rhythm authority.**
2. **Normalized rhythmic/note progress from 0.0 to 1.0.**
3. **Deterministic macro-cycle closure.**
4. **Exact Phase Zero realignment.**
5. **Trigger Engines receive timing/progress; they do not own musical time.**
6. **Trigger Engines express rhythm geometrically.**
7. **Visual geometry must not determine or destabilize musical timing.**
8. **Collision-derived note triggering is prohibited as an authoritative timing method.**
9. **Audio and visuals derive from shared musical state rather than maintaining independent clocks.**

These are design/architecture requirements, not proof that the current application already satisfies them.

### 3.5 Art-direction principles

1. **Monumental restraint with a cold, living palette.**
   - Strong directions include green, blue, cold white, blue-grey, fog, oxidized metal, moss, clouds, and overcast daylight.
2. **“Ancient future” is a governing principle.**
   - God-level or reality-altering technology coexists with old institutions, rites, guild-like continuity, inherited objects, elaborate clothing, and deep aesthetic tradition.
3. **MTC identity should feel like institutional archaeology, not startup branding.**
   - Think markings, seals, patches, manuals, certification stamps, aircraft applications, engraved materials, and systems that plausibly accumulate across generations.
4. **The visual world should avoid generic cyberpunk and generic evil-corporation codes.**
5. **Mathematical harmony is a valid generator of visual language.**
   - Chladni patterns, standing-wave interference, Lissajous figures, Fourier forms, and spherical/polar harmonics are established design sources.
6. **Gliders should not read as ordinary futuristic fighter jets.**
   - Their identity is quiet, efficient, current-riding, high-altitude, and civilizationally necessary.

## 4. PLANNED

These systems have clear support as intended directions, but their contracts and launch scope are not fixed.

### 4.1 Persistent world weather

- One world-scale weather system should continue independently of any individual player.
- Conditions span regions and transit routes.
- Players check forecasts to plan future crossings.
- Weather can affect:
  - route availability and closures;
  - danger;
  - visibility and visual atmosphere;
  - sound and harmonic density;
  - transit duration;
  - Trigger Engine/compositional behavior;
  - anomaly encounters.
- Tailwinds shortening a crossing and headwinds lengthening it are strong examples, not yet a complete formula.
- The simulation should be authoritative/shared rather than independently randomized in every browser.
- The world should continue changing while players are offline.

**Open design risk:** If transit duration changes, the musical system must preserve deterministic structure and Phase Zero closure. Weather cannot be allowed to create an unrelated timing authority.

### 4.2 Progression

- The player begins as a Navigator or Navigator-in-training and develops toward exceptional mastery.
- Progression should affect more than numerical power:
  - glider equipment/technology;
  - audiovisual richness;
  - Trigger Engine presentation;
  - available sounds, notes, layers, or sound packs;
  - cockpit/HUD/world “skin”;
  - access to routes and deeper anomalous perception.
- A high-level transit should be visibly and musically richer than an early transit.
- Progression should reveal more of the world rather than only increasing stats.

**Unfixed:** level scale, rank names, skill trees, classes, colors, exact upgrade economy, death penalties, and whether becoming Meridian is the endpoint.

### 4.3 Narrative through transmissions

- Transits should reveal the world through transmissions and fragments rather than exposition dumps.
- The tonal mix should include routine operational communication, human moments, anomaly reports, institutional messages, and rare revelations.
- Ordinary material is important because it makes breaks in professionalism and abnormal events more powerful.
- Narrative discovery should be gradual and compatible with ambient play.

### 4.4 Data and information transit

- Fragmented civilization depends on MTC for transport and continued connection.
- Physical transport of information, data, or components needed to sustain a future communication network is a strong premise.

**Unfixed:** the “quantum computer/internet” explanation, what exactly must be physically carried, and the boundary between cargo, data courier work, and passenger transit.

### 4.5 Procedural transit environments

- The preferred long-term approach is a reusable real-time/procedural sky environment rather than a separate long prerecorded video for every route.
- Route, world, weather, time-of-day, altitude, biome, and anomaly parameters should shape the view.
- The environment should remain visually compelling over 10–90 minute crossings.

**Unfixed:** web-native renderer, Unreal, TouchDesigner, prerendered layers, hybrid pipeline, performance budget, and asset-production workflow.

### 4.6 Community systems preserving solitude

- Asynchronous connection is preferred before intrusive real-time social layers.
- The community may collectively document anomalies, routes, forecasts, and discoveries.
- Player-generated knowledge should not automatically become objective canon.
- Social features must not overwhelm the composed audio or meditative isolation.

### 4.7 Artifact provenance / inheritance

- Rare old MTC objects passed from player to player are a strong planned mechanic and cultural tradition.
- Each artifact may retain provenance such as prior holders, journeys, distance, routes, events, and age.
- The current holder eventually chooses a successor.
- The design purpose is emotional continuity and “joint solitude,” not loot rarity alone.

**Unfixed:** acquisition rules, rewards, loss conditions, eligibility, transfer timing, whether holders communicate, and whether objects physically change.

## 5. EXPLORATORY

The following ideas may be valuable inputs to later runs but are not current canon.

## 7. SUPERSEDED / REJECTED

1. **MTC as a corrupt Weyland-Yutani-style corporation**
   - Rejected by the creator in favor of a good, necessary civilizational institution.

2. **Kashmer-Alterra as the planet replacing Earth**
   - Rejected by clarification that the planet is still Earth.

3. **“Pilot” as the preferred in-world profession**
   - Superseded by **Navigator**, though “pilot” remains acceptable explanatory shorthand.

4. **Meridian as merely an ordinary rank**
   - The simple “Navigator → Meridian → Master Meridian” ladder was challenged in favor of Meridian as something a person becomes or is recognized as. The replacement is not fully locked.

5. **Attuned as the profession name**
   - Proposed but not selected; later terminology centered on Navigator and Meridian.

6. **Navigators as generic magic users/Jedi-like figures**
   - Rejected as a creative direction. Their extraordinary qualities should be grounded in trained perception, physiology, technical practice, altered physics, and ambiguous spirituality.

7. **Strong narcotic/psychedelic dependence as the defining Navigator mechanism**
   - De-emphasized in favor of perception and calibration. It may be revisited, but is not current canon.

8. **Generic fighter-jet/spacecraft gliders**
   - Rejected direction. Craft should visibly embody gliding, efficiency, silence, and functional continuity.

9. **Overly ceremonial/fantasy-priest Meridian attire**
   - Explicitly criticized as insufficiently rugged and human for a late-stage apocalypse. Future attire should retain sacred/institutional weight while reading as working equipment.

10. **Purely corporate, nautical, airline, navy, shipping, compass, globe, or wing branding**
    - Rejected as the main MTC identity direction.

11. **Clean contemporary startup/Swiss branding as the whole identity**
    - Superseded by institutional archaeology, accumulated markings, ritual utility, and mathematically generated symbolism.

12. **Logo mathematics used only as annotation**
    - Rejected. Mathematics should generate or materially determine form rather than be placed decoratively beside an arbitrary symbol.

13. **Long prerecorded route videos as the default content model**
    - Superseded as the preferred long-term direction by a reusable procedural or hybrid sky system, though prerendered elements remain possible.

14. **Independent client-side weather randomization**
    - Rejected for the living-world vision in favor of shared authoritative world state.

15. **Weather as merely a cosmetic effect**
    - Superseded by weather as a strategic, audiovisual, temporal, and world-simulation system.

16. **Random artifact loot with a known rarity percentage as the core artifact design**
    - Superseded by provenance, mystery, stewardship, and deliberate passing between real players.

17. **Horror defined primarily by darkness, gore, monsters, or jump scares**
    - Rejected by the beautiful/daylight/controlled-sublime horror direction.

18. **“Degradation” as the only description of altered matter**
    - Superseded creatively by **convergence/transformation**, which does not presume simple decay.
