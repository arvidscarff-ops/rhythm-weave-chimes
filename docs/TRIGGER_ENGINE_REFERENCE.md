## 1. Purpose

This document preserves a foundational fact that was absent from the initial Project Bible:

> **The audiovisual polyrhythm work of Lucid Rhythms is the originating technical and experiential reference for PHASE’s Trigger Engine system.**

PHASE began as an attempt to create an independently implemented browser-based generative audiovisual instrument inspired by the capabilities demonstrated in Lucid Rhythms’ work. The world, transit, game, and progression concepts developed later.

The reference remains important after that expansion. Trigger Engines are not incidental visualizers inside the future game. They must first succeed as compelling generative audiovisual compositions that can be enjoyed for:

- attentive listening and viewing;
- ambient music;
- meditation;
- deep work and concentration;
- rest;
- long-form pattern observation.

The PHASE world is a deeper layer revealed through and around that successful instrument.

## 2. Reference status and evidence boundary

### 2.1 What is selected

The project owner selected the six works in Section 3 as the initial canonical reference corpus for Trigger Engine study.

“Canonical reference corpus” means that PHASE should study and evaluate the capabilities those works demonstrate. It does not make their visual identity, audio, compositions, terminology, software design, or implementation part of PHASE canon.

### 2.2 What is confirmed

Public creator descriptions establish that Lucid Rhythms’ work involves:

- generative music;
- patterns and structures;
- chance operations;
- polyrhythms;
- long-form listening;
- relaxation;
- concentration;
- creativity;
- custom software made as an art project.

The selected titles and published durations establish that several references are presented as multi-hour works.

### 2.3 What is not known

PHASE does not have:

- Lucid Rhythms’ source code;
- an authoritative specification of its algorithms;
- its internal clock, scheduler, voice, event, rendering, or persistence design;
- permission to reuse its audiovisual content;
- proof that visible contact or collision authoritatively causes audio;
- proof of how chance operations are seeded;
- proof of how full-cycle duration is calculated;
- proof of which production or postprocessing tools are used.

Technical explanations derived from viewing remain **INFERENCE** until independently verified. PHASE’s implementation must obey its own accepted architecture even if a reference appears to behave differently.

## 4. Capability target

The reference corpus establishes a capability ambition, not an instruction to reproduce six works.

A PHASE Trigger Engine system should eventually support:

1. **Immediate standalone value.** A first-time user should encounter an audiovisual composition worth watching and hearing without first understanding the world or creating an account.
2. **Long-form credibility.** Engines must support sessions substantially longer than a short demo loop without obvious repetition, drift, instability, or exhausting presentation.
3. **Multiple voice relationships.** Independent rhythmic voices should remain perceptible as parts of one musical system.
4. **Visible musical truth.** Geometry should make actual rhythmic relationships legible.
5. **Deterministic closure.** Participating voices must return exactly to their defined relationship.
6. **Distinct engine families.** Variants should express materially different spatial and compositional ideas rather than recoloring one mechanism.
7. **Musical sound design.** Scales, tuning, timbre, dynamics, register, humanization, ambience, and layering must support prolonged listening.
8. **Bounded generativity.** Chance operations may create variation, but their scope, seed, reconstruction, and gameplay relevance must be explicit.
9. **Foreground and peripheral use.** The same underlying composition should support attentive observation, meditation, work, rest, and re-entry after attention shifts.
10. **Authorable configuration.** My Studio should be able to create and validate scales, sound packs, compositions, and permitted Trigger Engine configuration without bypassing architectural invariants.
11. **World integration without dependency.** Transit and environmental systems may situate and modulate compositions through governed contracts, but the engine remains valuable before those systems exist.
12. **Export readiness.** A future capture pipeline should be able to identify the exact engine, composition, configuration, seed, and event history used for a recording.

## 5. Required PHASE interpretation

### 5.1 Musical events remain authoritative

Visible impact, collision, crossing, or contact may represent an event. Rendered geometry never decides authoritative note timing.

The logical relationship is:

```text
authoritative rhythmic state
            │
            ▼
      musical event identity
          ┌─┴─┐
          ▼   ▼
       audio  visual expression
```

This preserves the perceptual effect of visible rhythm while remaining deterministic, frame-rate-independent, and reconstructable.

### 5.2 The gateway is not a throwaway demo

The first instrument surface is part of the finished product identity. It should not become a temporary technical sandbox that disappears when the world layer arrives.

World discovery may deepen:

- where a composition comes from;
- what an engine represents;
- which routes, institutions, conditions, or histories shaped it;
- what sounds, geometries, or capabilities become available;
- why precision and closure matter in fiction.

The world should add meaning without retroactively making the standalone experience incomplete.

### 5.3 My Studio is initially internal

During Reset / PHASE 2.0, My Studio is an owner/developer authoring tool.

Its player-facing future is unresolved. Its immediate purpose is to create, preview, validate, version, and publish PHASE content safely.

## 6. Originality, rights, and attribution boundary

Lucid Rhythms is an acknowledged inspiration and reference.

PHASE must not:

- copy or redistribute Lucid Rhythms audio or video;
- reproduce exact compositions;
- extract or reuse visual assets;
- imitate branding, titles, thumbnails, or presentation as product identity;
- claim access to or reconstruction of the private source code;
- market PHASE as official, affiliated, endorsed, or identical software;
- treat a visible reference behavior as permission to copy protected expression.

PHASE may independently develop general capabilities and ideas such as:

- polyrhythmic composition;
- deterministic phase relationships;
- mathematical audiovisual representation;
- generative music;
- pendulum, radial, linear, orbital, lattice, or path-based geometry;
- long-form ambient presentation;
- independently designed sound, interaction, rendering, and authoring systems.

Every production reference analysis should record:

1. what principle is being studied;
2. what PHASE transforms;
3. what must not be copied;
4. what remains uncertain;
5. how the result will be evaluated independently.

## 7. Reset / PHASE 2.0 implications

The selected reference corpus must be studied before the current application shell is treated as a permanent product structure.

Reset should:

1. preserve useful timing, sound, scene, Studio, and data work;
2. classify every current surface as preserve, refactor, replace, reference temporarily, or remove after verification;
3. establish a clean instant-entry instrument surface;
4. prove one correct authoritative event path;
5. deliver a small family of materially distinct Trigger Engines;
6. make My Studio reliable as a private authoring tool;
7. remove superseded code only after replacement behavior is verified;
8. begin game-graphics experiments from the stable audiovisual foundation.

Reset must not:

- delete valuable experiments before inventory;
- use the references to justify derivative copying;
- build final game UI before the entry instrument works;
- hide architectural timing defects beneath visual polish;
- promote user-authored Trigger Engines from EXPLORATORY to committed scope;
- treat six references as six required replicas.

## 8. Evaluation questions

For each PHASE reference engine:

- Is the musical rule understandable through sound and motion?
- Does the engine remain interesting after ten minutes?
- Does it remain comfortable during work, meditation, or rest?
- Can a returning viewer perceive larger cycles and approaching closure?
- Do different voices remain distinguishable?
- Does variation feel bounded and composed?
- Is the exact current state reconstructable?
- Does frame rate affect only smoothness?
- Does the engine have an identity beyond palette and particle count?
- Would the experience still be valuable without lore or progression?
- Can world context later deepen it without replacing it?
- Is the result recognizably PHASE rather than a Lucid Rhythms imitation?
