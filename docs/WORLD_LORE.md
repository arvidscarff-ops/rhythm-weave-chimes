# PHASE World Lore

**Document role:** World bible  
**Run:** 5 of the PHASE Project Bible documentation plan  
**Authority:** Canon and mystery framework for the fictional setting  
**Working setting label:** Kashmer-Alterra — **UNRESOLVED**, not a locked final universe title  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-02

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines what is known about the world of PHASE while protecting its mysteries from accidental explanation.

Every significant claim is assigned to one of four truth layers:

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **OBJECTIVE CANON** | True in the fictional world as currently established by the creator. |
| **IN-WORLD KNOWLEDGE** | Reliably observed or institutionally accepted by inhabitants, but not necessarily ultimate truth. |
| **IN-WORLD BELIEF / FOLKLORE** | A belief, theory, rumor, ritual interpretation, or disputed account held inside the world. |
| **CREATOR-UNRESOLVED** | The project has not decided the answer. Later writers must not fill the gap casually. |

An additional label appears where needed:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **EXPLORATORY** | A brainstormed possibility available for later selection, not current canon. |

### Governing rule

> **Characters may believe more than the creators have decided, and institutions may confidently describe phenomena whose ultimate nature remains unknown.**

This distinction permits deep lore without collapsing mystery.

## 2. Minimum canonical world statement

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

PHASE is set on Earth approximately 130 years after an extraterrestrial arrival or contact event began transforming the planet.

The first **Zones** formed in association with that arrival. Over the following generations, the transformation spread across or came to affect most of Earth. Matter, biology, weather, and possibly space and time now exhibit anomalous behavior connected to harmony and rhythm.

Human civilization survives in fragmented communities. Long-distance movement remains difficult and essential. **Meridian Transit Corporation (MTC)** maintains the glider-based transit network that connects surviving places. Its **Navigators** are highly trained specialists able to operate in harmonic and atmospheric conditions ordinary people cannot reliably read or endure.

The world is damaged but not socially or morally empty. People maintain institutions, routes, equipment, records, rituals, meals, relationships, and forecasts. Humanity does not know whether it will recover. It continues anyway.

## 4. Chronological framework

The exact calendar and dates are unresolved. The chronology below uses relative eras.

### Era 0 — Before arrival

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Earth existed in a recognizably human technological civilization before the extraterrestrial event.

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

- exact historical divergence from our world;
- date of arrival;
- political state;
- climate state;
- level of technology;
- whether MTC already existed;
- whether harmonic research predated contact;
- whether humanity detected the visitors before arrival.

### Era 1 — Arrival/contact

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Something extraterrestrial reached or touched down on Earth.

The first Zones began in connection with this event.

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

- number of craft/entities;
- whether “landing” is technically accurate;
- whether contact was intentional;
- whether the visitors were alive;
- whether humans communicated with them;
- whether they remained;
- whether arrival and transformation occurred simultaneously;
- whether humanity understood the event as extraterrestrial at the time.

### Era 2 — First Zones

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Areas of anomalous transformation formed and expanded or multiplied.

Matter, life, atmosphere, and physical relationships began behaving differently.

**IN-WORLD KNOWLEDGE**

Early institutions would have observed patterns, established exclusion procedures, and attempted classification.

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

- pace of expansion;
- casualties;
- whether governments collapsed quickly or gradually;
- whether Zones move;
- whether all Zones share one cause;
- whether “Zone” was the original term.

### Era 3 — Fragmentation and adaptation

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Over generations, most of Earth became engulfed, transformed, or strongly affected. Human communities became geographically fragmented. Reliable transit became essential.

**IN-WORLD KNOWLEDGE**

People learned that some paths and conditions could be crossed with specialized gliders, instrumentation, and trained Navigators.

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

- exact population;
- continental geography;
- sea level/climate;
- condition of orbit and satellites;
- survival of nation-states;
- whether surface, sea, or underground travel remains viable;
- how the first transit network formed.

### Era 4 — Present day

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Approximately 130 years have passed since arrival/contact.

No living society remembers the pre-arrival world through ordinary personal memory alone. Knowledge persists through institutions, records, inherited objects, architecture, and culture.

MTC and its Navigators form a central part of long-distance continuity.

The transformed world is not a temporary emergency. It is the environment in which multiple generations have lived.

## 5. The visitors

### 5.1 What is objectively established

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

- The initiating presence was extraterrestrial.
- Its arrival/contact is associated with the first Zones.
- Something associated with it now permeates or affects Earth.

### 5.2 What is not established

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

The project has not decided:

- species;
- appearance;
- number;
- biology;
- consciousness;
- motive;
- origin;
- destination;
- whether they are still present;
- whether a “ship” was a vehicle, organism, instrument, seed, habitat, or misclassification;
- whether the transformation was deliberate;
- whether humans were noticed;
- whether the visitors altered Earth or merely exposed a latent property of reality.

### 5.3 Mystery boundary

The visitors must not become a conventional alien antagonist by default.

The world should support the possibility that:

- humans are observing side effects rather than intent;
- categories such as invasion, experiment, accident, and colonization are insufficient;
- the transformation has a coherent purpose inaccessible to human scale;
- what arrived may not distinguish biology, technology, sound, and environment as humans do.

These are **possibilities**, not answers.

### 5.4 In-world theories

**IN-WORLD BELIEF / FOLKLORE — EXPLORATORY**

Plausible schools of thought may include:

- **contamination theory:** alien matter or biology spread from landing sites;
- **instrument theory:** the craft activated a planetary resonance system;
- **revelation theory:** harmony always governed reality and contact made it accessible;
- **terraforming theory:** Earth is converging toward conditions suitable for another form of life;
- **passage theory:** the visitors only passed through and the Zones are wake effects;
- **translation theory:** Earth is being converted into a different physical language;
- **memory theory:** repeated conditions imply the atmosphere or planet is replaying information.

No theory is canonically correct.

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

It may be:

- material;
- biological;
- informational;
- field-like;
- atmospheric;
- dimensional;
- a change in physical law;
- a human description of several interacting phenomena.

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE CANON**

- beautiful;
- productive;

## 7. Harmony as physics

### 7.1 Established premise

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Harmony and rhythm have material significance in the transformed world. They enable or mediate effects beyond conventional music, including manipulation or alteration of matter and potentially space and time.

### 7.2 What humanity can do

**IN-WORLD KNOWLEDGE**

Human institutions have learned enough to:

- identify repeatable harmonic relationships;
- build or operate transit instrumentation;
- navigate certain corridors/conditions;
- train Navigators;
- maintain gliders;
- use musical/rhythmic structure as more than aesthetic expression.

The precise degree of manipulation remains open.

### 7.3 Scientific model

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

Harmony may be:

- a new fifth-force-like interaction;
- a control language for altered matter;
- a way of measuring an underlying field;
- a property of reality newly made accessible;
- an emergent interface between biology, atmosphere, and visitor technology;
- an incomplete human model that works operationally without being ultimately true.

### 7.4 Limits

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

No canon limits have been established for:

- range;
- energy;
- precision;
- cost;
- reversibility;
- biological risk;
- space/time effects;
- who can perform manipulation;
- whether technology or the human body is primary;
- whether harmony creates effects or aligns with existing ones.

### 7.5 Operational truth versus ultimate truth

MTC may possess formulas that reliably predict safe transit without understanding why they work.

This distinction should be central to the world:

> A procedure can be correct without its explanation being complete.

### 7.6 432 Hz

**OBJECTIVE PROJECT CANON**

The musical world uses a 432 Hz tuning reference.

**SAFE TECHNICAL INTERPRETATION**

This most plausibly means A4 = 432 Hz, not that every note has the same frequency.

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

In fiction, 432 Hz may be:

- an MTC standard;
- a pre-arrival tuning tradition;
- the most stable post-arrival reference;
- a property of altered physics;
- a cultural belief later validated or mythologized;
- an artistic rule that does not require lore exposition.

Do not claim real-world mystical or scientific properties for 432 Hz.

## 10. Meridian Transit Corporation

### 10.1 Identity

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

The central institution is **Meridian Transit Corporation (MTC)**.

MTC is necessary and broadly benevolent. It is not defined by secret corruption.

### 10.2 Function

**OBJECTIVE CANON / PLANNED DETAIL**

MTC is responsible for much of humanity’s long-distance continuity.

Its sphere includes:

- transit routes;
- gliders;
- navigation;
- training;
- certification;
- maintenance;
- harmonic infrastructure;
- operational communication;
- weather/route decisions;
- synchronization of separated communities.

The exact legal ownership of these systems is unresolved.

### 10.3 Institutional character

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE CANON**

MTC should feel:

- old;
- dependable;
- technically rigorous;
- culturally important;
- ritualized through repetition;
- marked by inherited practice;
- more like a public utility or civilizational service than an airline.

It may be legally a corporation while culturally functioning as something larger.

### 10.4 History

**STRONG DIRECTION, CREATOR-UNRESOLVED FACT**

The key formulation is:

> A European scientific institution founded in the 1960s that survived into roughly 2150.

This may be:

- literal founding history;
- a visual/naming brief;
- a combination of precursor institution and later MTC.

### 10.6 Public language

## 11. Navigators

### 11.1 Definition

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

A Navigator is a human trained to perform transit through altered atmospheric and harmonic conditions.

“Navigator” is preferred to “pilot.”

### 11.2 What makes them distinct

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Navigators do more than operate ordinary aircraft.

They are able to:

- perceive or interpret subtle harmonic conditions;
- maintain coherence in dangerous transit space;
- make continuous corrections;
- read atmospheric/resonant change;
- use specialized instrumentation and trained bodily practice.

### 11.3 Training

**IN-WORLD KNOWLEDGE / PLANNED**

Training plausibly includes:

- listening;
- rhythmic precision;
- breathing;
- calibration;
- visual/perceptual exercises;
- route study;
- glider systems;
- weather interpretation;
- anomaly procedure.

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

- duration;
- age of entry;
- selection process;
- innate aptitude;
- institutional schools;
- failure rate;
- licensure;
- apprenticeship;
- substances or implants.

### 11.4 Technical practice perceived as ritual

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE CANON**

Navigator practices may look spiritual to outsiders while remaining operationally necessary.

Breathing before launch may be calibration. Silence may protect concentration. Repeated gestures may verify rhythm or equipment. Over generations, precise procedures may accumulate ceremony, reverence, and symbolism.

The world need not choose cleanly between scientific and sacred interpretations.

### 11.5 Social regard

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Navigators are regarded with near-holy respect because civilization depends on them and few people can do what they do.

They are not canonically clergy or gods.

### 11.6 Daily reality

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE DIRECTION**

The mythology around Navigators should coexist with exhaustion, work, maintenance, error, routine, and human limitation.

A child may see a saint. The Navigator may feel only fatigue and responsibility.

### 11.7 Cost of the vocation

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

Possible costs include:

- physiological adaptation;
- social isolation;
- sensory overload;
- slow recovery;
- memory effects;
- shortened career;
- altered emotional expression;
- increased compatibility with Zones;
- difficulty returning to ordinary environments.

No cost is selected.

## 18. Organisms

**OBJECTIVE CANON AT CATEGORY LEVEL**

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE DIRECTION**

**EXPLORATORY**

It should avoid becoming a direct SCP Foundation analogue.

Its records may be clinical, but the institution belongs to a lived civilization, not an infinite anomaly-content machine.

## 20. Religion, spirituality, and belief

### 20.1 Current canon

**OBJECTIVE CANON**

Navigators receive near-sacred regard. Harmony has material consequences. Technical practices may accumulate ritual significance.

### 20.2 What is not canon

There is no established:

- world religion;
- Navigator church;
- cult of MTC;
- divine truth;
- prophecy;
- pantheon;
- confirmed soul/metaphysics.

### 20.3 Productive ambiguity

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE DIRECTION**

PHASE should support **scientific spirituality**:

- awe without proof of divinity;
- ritual grounded in repeated consequence;
- beauty generated by physical order;
- institutions using sacred language because ordinary language is insufficient;
- people disagreeing about whether harmony is discovered, imposed, or alive.

### 20.4 Possible belief systems

**EXPLORATORY**

Communities might believe:

- the visitors tuned Earth;
- the world is converging toward a final chord;
- Navigators sacrifice part of themselves to keep routes open;
- certain repeated weather is memory;
- Meridians no longer have individual rhythm;
- objects gain meaning through the routes they survive.

These should remain beliefs unless selected as objective truth.

## 22. Culture and tradition

### 22.1 Ancient future

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE CANON**

The future has had time to become old.

Technology coexists with:

- lineage;
- rites;
- craft;
- institutional language;
- old symbols;
- material repair;
- inherited equipment;
- ceremonial recognition;
- accumulated superstition.

### 22.2 Tradition as compressed knowledge

A tradition may persist because:

- its reason remains understood;
- its reason was forgotten but the practice still works;
- it once worked but no longer does;
- it carries identity independent of utility.

Worldbuilding should not automatically mock tradition as irrational.

### 22.3 Naming language

**PLANNED CREATIVE DIRECTION**

Names should feel:

- pronounceable in Swedish, English, and German;
- European;
- partly Scandinavian/Latin;
- institutional rather than generic sci-fi;
- free of fantasy apostrophes and obvious tech-startup jargon.

This is a naming brief, not an in-world language map.

### 22.4 Attire

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE DIRECTION**

Navigator/Meridian attire should combine:

- rugged working equipment;
- human wear;
- late-apocalyptic practicality;
- institutional ritual;
- inherited or repaired elements;
- technical purpose.

Overly pristine fantasy-priest ceremony is rejected.

## 26. Beautiful horror in-world

### 26.1 Human adaptation

After 130 years, people have procedures and language for conditions that remain cosmically strange.

This produces a specific horror:

- impossible phenomena appear in forecasts;
- dangerous beauty receives an operational code;
- children grow up knowing rules whose origins they do not understand;
- professionals discuss catastrophe in calm voices;
- a route may be closed because the sky is behaving too perfectly.

### 26.2 No universal panic

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE CANON**

People are not constantly screaming.

They work.

When calm breaks, it matters.

### 26.3 No simple victory

**CREATOR-UNRESOLVED**

Humanity’s ultimate prospects are unknown.

The setting does not currently promise:

- cure;
- reversal;
- victory over visitors;
- restoration of Earth;
- inevitable extinction.

### 26.4 Hope

**OBJECTIVE CREATIVE CANON**

Hope is small but real.

It exists as action:

- maintaining;
- crossing;
- recording;
- teaching;
- feeding;
- repairing;
- handing something forward.

## 27. Deliberately preserved mysteries

The following should remain mysterious unless a later decision explicitly resolves them:

1. What exactly arrived?
2. Did it intend to change Earth?
3. Is it still present?
4. What is the transformation converging toward?
5. Is harmony fundamental physics or a human control model?
6. Why can some humans navigate altered conditions?
7. What distinguishes a Navigator from a possible Meridian?
8. Are repeated patterns memory, simulation, or coincidence?
9. Do Zones have agency?
10. Can humanity survive indefinitely?
11. Does MTC understand more than it publicly states?
12. Are the most beautiful phenomena invitations, side effects, or living things?

“Preserved mystery” does not mean no internal answer may ever exist. It means writers and agents must not resolve the question casually while implementing unrelated content.

## 28. Lore anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Why it conflicts |
|---|---|
| Aliens explained as straightforward invaders | Collapses cosmic ambiguity |
| Zones copied from *Roadside Picnic* or *Annihilation* | Replaces PHASE’s transformation logic with reference material |
| MTC secretly evil by default | Contradicts the benevolent necessary-institution canon |
| Navigator as generic wizard | Loses technical, perceptual, and bodily grounding |
| Meridian as ordinary level cap | Flattens an unresolved transformative concept |
| Every anomaly attacks | Turns ecology/mystery into enemy content |
| Gore as primary alteration language | Contradicts beautiful daylight horror |
| “Corruption” everywhere | Presumes moral decay rather than convergence |
| Invented science stated as settled fact | Removes in-world uncertainty |
| Scientific record equals objective truth | Institutions can be operationally right and ultimately incomplete |
| Community wiki equals canon | Erases authority layers |
| Constant misery | Makes routine, sincerity, and hope impossible |
| Medieval culture pasted over sci-fi | Ancient future must emerge from history and function |
| 432 Hz pseudoscience | Confuses artistic/world rule with real-world claims |
| Forty regions treated as committed scope | Promotes exploratory scale |

## 30. Writing rules for future lore

1. Label objective truth, institutional knowledge, belief, and unresolved authorial questions.
2. Prefer observation before explanation.
3. Let procedures reveal history.
4. Keep extraordinary events sparse enough to remain extraordinary.
5. Include ordinary human life in every major location or institution.
6. Never make technical language meaningless decoration.
7. Do not use mystery to excuse contradiction.
8. Do not answer a preserved mystery in flavor text accidentally.
9. Let institutions be sincere, competent, and still incomplete.
10. Make altered nature coherent without making it morally evil.
11. Keep Navigators human even when others regard them as sacred.
12. Treat objects as carriers of history.
13. Use references as principles, never as lore templates.
14. Do not lock image-dependent designs without the missing reference assets.
15. Record any new canon decision in `DECISIONS.md` when that document exists.
